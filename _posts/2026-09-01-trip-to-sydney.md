## Trip to Sydney

In July, I went to Sydney to attend RSS2026. This week-long trip to Sydney left me with unforgettable memories.

---

### 2026-07-12: Arrival in Sydney

We took off from Xiamen Airport on the evening of July 11th, and after a 9-hour flight, arrived at Sydney Kingsford Smith Airport on the morning of July 12th, Sydney time.

We then took a taxi to our hotel near Darling Harbour, but we had to wait until 3 pm to check in, even though we were exhausted. We still wandered around the area and had brunch. The weather in Sydney was fantastic today, very sunny, and although it's winter, it wasn't cold at all.

![Sydney](/images/2026090102.jpeg)

![Sydney](/images/2026090103.jpeg)

That time happened to be the World Cup match between Argentina and Switzerland. There was a group viewing event organized near Darling Harbour, and we watched the game from the terrace next to the stadium.

![Sydney](/images/2026090104.jpeg)

A lively amusement park by Darling Harbour.

![Sydney](/images/2026090105.jpeg)

We finally checked in. We started catching up on sleep, sleeping from the afternoon until evening.

![Sydney](/images/2026090106.jpeg)

After getting up in the evening, we decided to find something to eat. Our accommodation was near an area with many bars and restaurants, just a short walk away. We chose a restaurant called *Hello Auntie*, which mainly served Vietnamese food.

![Sydney](/images/2026090107.jpeg)

For our main dishes, we ordered rice noodles, steak, and corn, among other things.

![Sydney](/images/2026090108.jpeg)

We also ordered tiramisu for dessert.
